# Lip Light Glow — v1.6.4

- Inactive Lip Light Box voice LEDs are dull orange instead of gray.
- Audio-active LEDs ramp center-out to bright orange.
- Each LED has a separately driven emissive orange material.
- Each active LED gains a small translucent emissive halo.
- Four localized dynamic orange PointLights follow the active mouth regions and illuminate nearby head/mouth geometry.
- Point-light and halo intensity follow the same continuous audio amplitude used by the 14-LED display.
- No hardware/Live Drive behavior was changed.
