# URDF Pose Editor — v1.12.2

- Selecting a spline control point now selects its timeline time and refreshes the Commands-at-cursor list.
- Added a non-destructive Pose mode to the URDF viewer.
- Added a circular eye-gaze target, iris slider, flap/nose/neck/vent/eye-pop/whip/MFR/microphone direct-manipulation handles.
- Added LR Joined (default) and LR Split modes for symmetric/asymmetric eye and flap posing.
- Added timeline **Insert Pose** to convert the visible URDF pose into normal ganged or individual child commands.
- Pose drafts survive dock/undock; leaving Pose mode without insertion restores the timeline pose.
